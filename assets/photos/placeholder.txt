📸 PHOTO INSTRUCTIONS
======================

Place your photos in this folder with these exact filenames:
  - photo1.jpg
  - photo2.jpg
  - photo3.jpg
  - photo4.jpg
  - photo5.jpg

TIPS:
  • Portrait (vertical) or landscape photos both work — the carousel will crop them to fill the frame.
  • Recommended resolution: at least 800×600 px for crisp display.
  • JPG / PNG / WEBP are all supported — just update the filenames in CONFIG.photos inside script.js.
  • To add more photos, add more filenames to the CONFIG.photos array in script.js.

If a photo is missing, the carousel will show a cute placeholder instead — no errors! 🌸
